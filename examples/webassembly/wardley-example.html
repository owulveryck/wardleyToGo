Wardley Map Auto-Render Demo

This page demonstrates wardley.js: a drop-in script that automatically renders <pre><code class="language-wtg2"> blocks into SVG Wardley Maps. Just include the script and write your maps in fenced code blocks.

Tea Shop

A classic Wardley Map example:

title: Tea Shop

business - cup of tea
public - cup of tea
cup of tea - cup
cup of tea - tea
cup of tea - hot water
hot water - water
hot water - kettle
kettle - power
